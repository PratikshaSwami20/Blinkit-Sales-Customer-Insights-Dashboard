# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This is an end-to-end **Data Analytics project** developed using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI**. The project analyzes Blinkit's sales, customers, products, orders, delivery performance, inventory, and marketing campaigns through interactive dashboards. The objective is to help businesses monitor performance, identify trends, optimize operations, and make data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Blinkit handles thousands of customer orders, products, deliveries, and marketing campaigns every day. Managing this data manually makes it difficult to monitor sales performance, understand customer behavior, track inventory, evaluate delivery efficiency, and measure marketing effectiveness. This dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics to support better decision-making.

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 📈 Executive Overview

Provides a summary of overall business performance, including sales, orders, customers, products, and monthly sales trends.

### 👥📦 Customer & Product Analysis

Analyzes customer demographics, membership distribution, top-selling products, and category-wise sales.

### 🚚📦 Delivery & Inventory

Monitors delivery performance, order status, warehouse stock, inventory levels, and delivery across cities.

### 📢📈 Marketing Performance

Evaluates campaign performance, advertising spend, revenue, conversions, CTR, ROI, and platform effectiveness.

# 💡 Key Insights

* 💰 Generated **₹6.55M Total Sales** from **5K Orders**.
* 🥤 **Beverages** is the highest-performing product category.
* 🛒 **Juice, Tea, and Milk** are the top-selling products.
* 👥 Customer memberships are evenly distributed across Gold, Silver, and Platinum.
* 🚚 Average delivery time is **43 minutes**, with most orders successfully delivered.
* 📦 Inventory analysis highlights products that require timely restocking.
* 📢 Marketing campaigns generated **₹42.24M Revenue** from **₹15.22M Ad Spend**, achieving **177.48% ROI**.
* 🌍 Mumbai, Pune, and Nagpur are the top-performing cities.
